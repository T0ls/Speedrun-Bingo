Speedrun Bingo

Game Rules

How to Play

  • Complete the goals on the bingo grid.
  • You can use any game that fits the goal.
  • Time is running: the faster you are, the better.
  • Every tome you win you get a point.
  • You can spend your points to inflict a penalty on another player.
  • If the player you used the penalty on wins the game, he gets his point plus the cost of your penalty.

Restrinctions

  • Each goal must be completed on a different game.
  • The game you chosse must be played on a new save, no older saves.
  • Creative mode is not allowed in all games.
  • All goals must be complete in game, no Cutscene.
